I have been part of **BNDS-China** at [iGEM](https://igem.org/), the world's largest
synthetic-biology competition, since 2022 — as a member, as **student leader** of the 2024 team, and
as **instructor** of the 2025 and 2026 teams. The team won **gold medals** and finished among the
**top 10 high-school teams** in 2023, 2024, and 2025, and in 2025 also took **Best Measurement**
and **Best Part Collection**.

- **2024, student leader:** coordinated the team and developed a programmable probiotic platform to
  detect, monitor, and regulate gut metabolites to help treat or prevent gut dysbiosis. Oversaw
  inducible-promoter characterization, and built the **dry lab** — modeling genetic-circuit expression
  dynamics and using protein-structure prediction. Nominated for Best Wiki.
- **2025, instructor:** supervised lab and outreach work, and developed computational-biology
  solutions for a programmable probiotic platform for **feline gut health**.
- **2026, instructor:** instructing the current team (ongoing).
- Interviewed clinicians and synthetic biologists, organized conferences with 10+ teams, and planned
  the 20-person trip to the Grand Jamboree in Paris.
