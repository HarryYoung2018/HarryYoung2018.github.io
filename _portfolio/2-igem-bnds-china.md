I led and later instructed **BNDS-China** at [iGEM](https://igem.org/), the world's largest
synthetic-biology competition. Over two seasons the team won **gold medals** and finished among the
**top 10 high-school teams**.

- Devised the research direction (a probiotic platform to alleviate gut dysbiosis), contacted labs,
  and oversaw experiments including inducible-promoter characterization.
- Built the **dry-lab** work: modeled genetic-circuit expression dynamics and used protein-structure
  prediction tools.
- Ran human-practices outreach, interviewed clinicians and synthetic biologists, and organized
  inter-team conferences; joined the iGEM Jamboree in Paris.
- **Role:** club president, 2024 team leader, 2025 instructor
