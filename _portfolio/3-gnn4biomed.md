A transfer-learning project that builds a **GCN/RGCN model** to predict **drug–disease indication**
relations over the [PrimeKG](https://github.com/mims-harvard/PrimeKG) biomedical knowledge graph,
with pharmacogenomics (drug–genetic-variant–disease) as the next step.

- **Stack:** Python, PyTorch Geometric, relational graph convolutional networks
- **Code:** [github.com/HarryYoung2018/GNN4Biomed](https://github.com/HarryYoung2018/GNN4Biomed)
