**Role:** co-first author — proposed and benchmarked the method · **Status:** submitted to the NeurIPS 2026
Workshop on Dynamic Alignment in Human–AI Coupled Systems ([BiAlign](https://bialign-workshop.github.io/neurips2026)).

We study **memory retrieval for autonomous agents** — deciding which past experiences an agent
should recall to act well now. Instead of relying on raw semantic similarity, we train the
retriever *offline* with a **learned surrogate** that scores how much a candidate memory improves
downstream task success. Directly mentored by [Prof. Xue (Steve) Liu](https://cs.mcgill.ca/~xueliu/site/intro.html)
and [Ye Yuan](https://stevenyuan666.github.io/) in the [Cyber-Physical Intelligence Lab](https://cpil-lab.github.io/)
and [AAAAA Community](https://github.com/AAAAA-Academia-Attractions).
