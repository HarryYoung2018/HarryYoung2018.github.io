## Summary

**Herculean** is a benchmark for AI-agent capability in realistic **financial workflows**. It spans
four domains — **Trading, Hedging, Market Insights, and Auditing** — each implemented as a
standardized environment with domain-specific tools and explicit success criteria, so agents are
judged on completing a workflow rather than answering a question.

Evaluating frontier agents shows a clear split: they are relatively strong on **Trading** and
**Market Insights**, but degrade sharply on **Hedging** and **Auditing**, where success demands
long-horizon coordination, consistent state tracking, and structured verification.

A large multi-institution collaboration; I am one of ~55 authors.
